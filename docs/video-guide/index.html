60-second guide

Hunter Intelligence Platform

From a client brief to a branded, cited deck, in eight steps.

Step 1 of 8

All Projects

Do this
Sign in. You land on All Projects.
You get
Every project at a glance, with its brand logo, brief type and last edit.
Hunter · All Projects
Sign in → All Projects
Step 2 of 8

New Project

Do this
Click New Project. Enter the client, market and time period, then paste or upload the brief.
You get
Click Analyze Brief and the AI starts reading it.
Hunter · New Project
New Project → Analyze Brief
Step 3 of 8

Brief & Scope

Do this
Check what the AI understood: client, competitors, market and scope.
You get
A structured scope you can edit. Click Approve to move on.
Hunter · Brief & Scope
Review → Approve
Step 4 of 8

Background Research

Do this
Open Background Research and let it run.
You get
A cited news brief on the brand and its competitors. Download it as Word.
Hunter · Background Research
Research → Approve
Step 5 of 8

Search Strategy

Do this
Generate the search strategy and pick Broad, Balanced or Precise.
You get
Meltwater Boolean queries, scored for coverage and precision. Copy them or export to Excel.
Hunter · Search Strategy
Strategy → Approve
Step 6 of 8

Data Sources

Do this
Run each query in Meltwater and upload the export to its research question.
You get
Record counts, date range and sentiment for every file. Approve each dataset.
Hunter · Data Sources
Upload → Approve
Step 7 of 8

Answers

Do this
Click Go to Deliverable, then open the Overview tab.
You get
An answer to every research question, with the figures and the sources behind it.
Hunter · Deliverables · Overview
Deliverables → Overview
Step 8 of 8

Your Deck

Do this
Open the Presentation tab and check the quality results.
You get
A branded deck. Download it as PowerPoint or PDF, plus a Word brief and Excel tables.
Hunter · Deliverables · Presentation
Presentation → Download

You're ready.
